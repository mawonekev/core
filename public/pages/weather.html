Tanzania Seasons Guide

Plan around Tanzania's two rainy and two dry seasons.

Dec – Mar

Short Dry + Short Rains

Warm and increasingly humid. Good wildlife viewing near water sources.

Apr – May

Long Rains (Masika)

Heavy rains, lush landscapes. Some roads difficult. Lower tourist prices.

Jun – Oct

Long Dry Season (Kiangazi)

Best wildlife viewing. Cool and dry. Peak safari season.

Nov

Short Rains (Mvua Ndogo)

Brief showers, fewer crowds. Green scenery starts returning.
