Our Mission

Tanzania has hundreds of attractions — national parks, ancient gorges, coral reefs, mountain peaks, and cultural villages — yet most travel platforms bury them behind paywalls or present outdated, inaccurate data. Xenohuru exists to change that.

We believe accurate, open travel data benefits the entire ecosystem: tourists plan better trips, local operators get discovered, conservationists reach wider audiences, and Tanzania's natural heritage gets the attention it deserves.

Our Values

Open by Default

Every line of code is public. Every dataset is freely accessible. No paywalls, no locked features — ever.

Community First

Built by Tanzanians and travel enthusiasts worldwide. The platform improves because people care enough to contribute.

For Everyone

Travellers, researchers, developers, and locals — Xenohuru serves all audiences equally.

Conservation Aware

We surface accurate ecosystem and wildlife data to help visitors make responsible travel choices.

Trustworthy Data

GPS-verified locations, community-reviewed content, and transparent sourcing — accuracy is non-negotiable.

Developer Friendly

Public REST API, open data exports, and a codebase welcoming contributors of all skill levels.
